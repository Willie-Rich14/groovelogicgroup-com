Gerard “Gripp” Richardson, known on set as The Hip Hop Jib Op, is Managing Director of Groove Logic Group Inc. Its production company, Gripp Tight Productions LLC, has been making music and media since August 2005, and Gripp has been on the jib since December 2011.

That date is the start of everything. On December 15, 2011, he took a one-way flight from Greenville-Spartanburg to Los Angeles to start a film career, and he still celebrates it as his career birthday. Within a week of meeting the right people at a New Year’s party, he had taken a class, gotten a call, worked his first day on set, and booked his first paid job.

That first week set the pattern for the next fifteen years. More than 90% of his work has come through relationships, and many of those relationships are still solid today. Skills and talent built the ship. Relationships cut the channel it traveled.

He went to the 2014 Winter Olympics as an assistant and was called back directly for 2018, 2022 and 2026. He has run the jib for livestreams at Egypt’s pyramids with the Red Hot Chili Peppers (2019) and Maroon 5 (2022), every year at Cali Roots in Monterey, and on stages from the Hollywood Bowl and Red Rocks to Wrigley Field and Madison Square Garden, along with film and TV sets and church productions.
