1. The AI Assessment Snapshot (one-time)

What it is: one scoped diagnostic session plus a written Snapshot report and a report-review call, for the flat fee shown at booking ($997 as of this draft). Payment is collected up front via the Stripe link on the booking page.

What it is not: implementation, custom development, software purchase, integrations, data migration, or ongoing support. It is not legal, tax, financial, cybersecurity, or medical advice.

2. Rescheduling and cancellation — Snapshot

  • Reschedule: free, any time up to 24 hours before the scheduled session — just reply to the confirmation email.
  • Cancel with 24+ hours' notice: full refund, no questions asked.
  • Cancel with less than 24 hours' notice, or a no-show: the fee is retained, since the session time was held and prep work may already be underway. One exception can be made per client for a genuine emergency.
  • Once the session has happened: the fee is earned and the report will be delivered; it is not refunded for not liking the findings. (The findings themselves come with no promised outcome — see §1.)

3. The Groove Logic Growth System (recurring)

  • Billing: monthly, in advance, at the tier selected (Starter / Growth / Scale).
  • Term: month-to-month. No long-term contract by default.
  • Cancellation: cancel any time with 30 days' written notice (an email to info@groovelogicgroup.com is enough). Access continues through the end of the notice period; no partial-month refund for the notice period itself.
  • If you cancel: you may be offered the option to keep the underlying tools/data running at a reduced monthly fee instead of losing access outright — that offer, if made, will always be optional and clearly priced separately, never automatic.
  • Tier changes: you can move up or down a tier at your next billing date as your team size changes; no penalty either direction.

4. What we ask you not to share

Please do not send passwords, API keys, financial account numbers, government ID numbers, health information, or other sensitive/regulated records — during the Snapshot, the discovery process, or as part of the Growth System. If something like that is ever needed for a specific piece of work, we'll ask for it through a secure, purpose-built method at that time, not by email or in a form.

5. Privacy — what we collect and what we do with it

The intake form and booking flow collect: your name, business name, email, and a description of the workflow you want looked at. That information is used only to prepare for and deliver your Snapshot or Growth System service, and to follow up with you about it.

  • We don't sell your information or share it with third parties for their own marketing.
  • We don't add you to a newsletter or drip sequence from this form — you get a personal reply, and nothing else, unless you separately opt in to something else later.
  • Information you share during a Snapshot session about your business is used only to prepare your report, and is not shared outside Groove Logic Group without your permission.
  • You can ask at any time what we have on file for your business and ask us to delete it, by emailing info@groovelogicgroup.com.
