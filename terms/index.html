6. Taxes

Researched, not yet CPA-confirmed — treat as a strong starting point, not a filed position. South Carolina doesn’t tax most professional/consulting services outright — it taxes tangible personal property plus a specific, enumerated list of services (repair, certain communications services, and, by Department of Revenue ruling, software accessed remotely through a browser — “Application Service Provider” access — treated as a taxable communications service under SC Revenue Rulings 05-13 and 12-1).

Applied to these two offers specifically:

  • The Snapshot — a diagnostic session and a written report, no software access delivered — falls on the professional-services side and is very likely not taxable.
  • The Growth System — ongoing access to a hosted system through a browser — matches the ASP/SaaS pattern SC has specifically ruled taxable. Likely subject to sales tax.
  • South Carolina is destination-based: the rate charged depends on where the customer is located, not on Columbia. The state rate is 6%, and counties add up to 3% more locally — Richland County (Columbia) is 8% combined, but a client elsewhere in SC could sit at a different combined rate.
  • If a Growth System client ever also gets meaningful hands-on consulting bundled in, keeping that itemized as a separate line from the platform-access fee on the invoice is what SC guidance points to for keeping the consulting piece untaxed — bundling it in undifferentiated risks pulling the whole thing under the taxable side.

This is a solid, sourced starting point (see the session log for the source links), not a filed tax position — get it confirmed by your CPA once one’s engaged (Phase 0 of the charter), and before it’s treated as settled here.
